# Technology risk intern, Financial Audit IT, June to August 2026, Ulaanbaatar

Assessed the security operations center maturity of one of Mongolia's largest banks against the SOC-CMM framework, working with the EY Hong Kong team from on-site walkthroughs through to the closing report.

Tested the IT general controls on a mining company's accounting system, covering system access, change management and daily operations, and documented the audit workpapers.

Briefed Financial Audit IT managers on two studies: account-takeover fraud that cost Mongolian banks about US$190M in 2025, and the technology-audit regulation governing the team's engagements.

Spent the summer explaining technical findings to people who owned the risk but not the code, which is most of what an audit is.
